10/25/26 - Finch, Chase, Eigls

Score to obtain Informants -- taking them from the Crows, giving them a -2 status.
The crew is asked by a child named Skip to help find one of his fellow runners, Tommy, who had gone missing a few weeks ago. The two of them had overheard that the Grinders pushed the Crows out of a weapons deal with manufacturers in Lockport. They then attempted to steal some weapons from one of the Grinders' warehouses in the Docks without orders from the Crows.  Skip got cold feet and bailed, but Tommy went anyway and never returned.

The crew identified which of the three warehouses was the HQ by following a shipment the Grinders commandeered at the docks. They broke in at night and split up, with Chase starting a fire (and then causing an explosion) as a distraction while Finch and Clew rescued Tommy from the office he was held in.

After the score, Finch was interrogated by the Bluecoats and took a bruising but gave no information. Chase began teaching the kids how to fight.

The crew gained 4 runners that now served as informants including Skip and Tommy.
