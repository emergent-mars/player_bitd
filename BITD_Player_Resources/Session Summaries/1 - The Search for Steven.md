9/19/26 -- Finch, Chase, Egils, and Komoti

Tessa summoned the Pests to an abandoned warehouse with a single furnished room used for under-the-table negotiations. The report time was midnight, and only she and Evelyn were there. She requested the crew retrieve a small red ledger from a whistleblower--a former Lampblack named Steven--and replace it with a fake she provided.

The crew tracked Steven to a rundown apartment complex where he had been squatting in an unoccupied unit. They successfully swapped the ledger, and left him to be apprehended by the Bluecoats. (He turned up dead a week later.)

Back at the crew’s lair, Komoti unlocked the ledger for everyone, and found it contained a list of names, signatures, and thumb prints in dried blood along with a mysterious mark. Finch recognized some of the names—a Red Sash, a few Crows, one of the Dimmer Sisters, and a few casino and drug den owners not in the Lampblacks. Notably, none of the names were Lampblacks. Egils realized that the mark was a personal identifier of sorts, and Finch deduced that this was a list of people who have a Ghost Contract with whoever the mark represents. Komoti relocked the ledger, and it was like it was never opened in the first place.

The next day, the crew turned the ledger over to Tessa, and got paid. After the score, Finch planted rumors that the old woman who called the Bluecoats was paranoid and it was only chance that there was an actual intruder in an attempt to discredit her if she mentions meeting Finch and Komoti.
